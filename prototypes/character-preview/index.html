Three new Mibos — pick a direction

Each concept is fully animated and does the same teaching job as the current Mibo: a big front-facing mouth you can copy, with teeth and tongue for s, th and l. The mouth cycles through the sounds under each character. Use the buttons to make each one wave, hop or cheer.

Hand-drawn timing kept from the current puppet: body poses held at 6 fps, lines boiling at 3 fps, mouth swapping faster over the held body. Watercolour washes, coloured-pencil lines, paper grain.

How to choose: judge the mouth first — could a 4-year-old copy that shape off a phone screen? Then the silhouette at app-icon size, then which one your child would want to see again tomorrow. Tell me A, B or C (or what to mix) and I will build the winner into the puppet rig properly.
